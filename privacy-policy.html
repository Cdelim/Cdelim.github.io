Privacy Policy

Junction Jam: Traffic Puzzle · Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Junction Jam (the "Game"), published by Cem ("we", "us"), collects, why, and what choices you have. The Game does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other contact details in the Game.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, cosmetic items and settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the Game deletes this data. We do not receive it.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses the following services, which may collect information from your device as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource Ads) and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) – to show advertisements, measure them and prevent fraud. They may collect your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA where permitted), IP address, device model, operating system, language, app and ad-interaction data. See the Unity privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (Google) – to understand how the Game is played (for example which levels are started, cleared or skipped, and app version and device type) so we can improve difficulty and fix problems. It uses an app instance identifier and device information. See Firebase privacy information and the Google privacy policy.

These providers may combine this information with data from other apps and websites to show ads that may interest you, if you allow personalised ads.

3. How the information is used

  • Showing rewarded and interstitial advertisements, which keep the Game free.
  • Measuring how levels perform, finding difficulty problems and improving the Game.
  • Detecting fraud and keeping the services secure.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the information to identify you personally.

4. Your choices

  • Personalised ads: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Türkiye, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice in the Game's settings. Without consent you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • iOS tracking: on iOS, the Game asks for permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Removing ads: the optional "Remove Ads" purchase turns off interstitial ads.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or KVKK in Türkiye), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, most requests are best directed to the service providers above; you can also contact us and we will help.

6. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and transfers

Third-party providers keep data for the periods described in their policies (for example, Firebase Analytics keeps event data for a limited, configurable period). Their servers may be located outside your country, including in the United States.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
